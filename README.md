# How it works in 3 easy steps.
## Setup takes less than 2 minutes. Once configured, booking becomes effortless.

1--
Pre-save your details
Open the Kunal Softwares tool before booking time. Enter passenger names, age, berth choices, and your payment details.

2--
Log in manually to IRCTC
At 09:58 AM (for AC) or 10:58 AM (for Non-AC), log in to IRCTC with your own password and OTP. Your account stays safe.

3--
Instant 1-click autofill
When the clock strikes 10:00:00 AM, select your train. The software autofills everything in under a second so you can confirm your seat.
